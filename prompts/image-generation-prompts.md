# OG Street Wear Image Generation Prompts

This document contains 40 ready-to-use image-generation prompts for men and women clothing styles. Each prompt is tuned to highlight garments in your product cards, with the topwear or bottomwear shown in full scale.

## Usage
- Export a high-resolution image: `3000x4000` or `3200x4000` for full quality.
- Use the negative tokens with every prompt to avoid logos, watermarks, and extra people.
- Save files in `public/images/products/`.

## Common negative tokens (use with every prompt)
- Negative: watermark, logo, brand name, text, low-res, extra people, busy background, overexposed, underexposed

- `women-top-1.jpg` … `women-top-10.jpg`

Use the exact filenames above in your app once you replace the placeholder images.

## Notes
- If you are generating with a tool that supports aspect ratio, use `4:5`.
- You can use `face cropped` or `head out of frame` to keep focus on clothing.
- Keep backgrounds clean and minimal so the garment is the star.
